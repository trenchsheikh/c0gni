## Why Agents, Not Bots?

Traditional bots are reactive scripts. c0gni agents are autonomous entities that:

- **Learn from every trade** with persistent on-chain memory
- **Coordinate as swarms** for compound intelligence
- **Execute at machine speed** with sub-second decision making
- **Evolve strategies** based on market conditions
